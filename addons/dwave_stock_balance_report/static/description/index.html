FAQ

Frequently Asked Question

1. Can I generate the report for all products or just specific ones?
Yes. You can run the report for all products, all categories or filter it down to specific products or specific categories — whichever fits your need.
2. In which formats can I export the report?
The report can be exported as a print-ready PDF or a styled Excel (XLSX) file - both downloadable in a single click directly from the wizard.
3. Can I group the report by category instead of by product?
Yes. You can choose to group the report by Product or by Category.
4. What columns are included in the report?
Each report includes 8 columns: Date, Reference, Customer / Vendor, Opening Stock, Inbound / Purchase, Outbound / Sales, Damage and Closing Stock - giving you the full picture of every stock movement.
